Major Event
An unprecedented event that changes everything.
⚠️ Reveal the details — SPOILERS
This is Crisis on Infinite Earths, the biggest crossover in all of DC history. The entire Multiverse is rebooted from scratch — some realities disappear, others are merged. Dozens of characters from dozens of DC movies and shows across the franchise's entire history make cameos. It's an unprecedented event.

Episodes:
Arrow — Season 8 Episode 8
The Flash — Season 6 Episode 9
Supergirl — Season 5 Episode 9
Batwoman — Season 1 Episode 9
Legends of Tomorrow — Season 5 Episode 1

Titans, Stargirl, Smallville, Superman Returns and Batman (1989) also take part.
