Star Wars Deep DiveNovels & Comics

If you want to get into the Star Wars novels and comics, you're in the right place. I'm assuming that if you're here, you've already watched the animated and live-action media.

The omitted media are the same as on the main timeline, except The Acolyte, which may be worth watching if you read the comics: it's the only screen production (besides Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures) set during the High Republic.

The omitted comics are throwaway one-shots, comics aimed at young children, or adaptations of screen productions.
