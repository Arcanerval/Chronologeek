Your data

Privacy policy

Updated November 14, 2026

In short

Chronologeek has no accounts, no data server and no advertising cookies. Your progress, your own additions and your settings stay in your browser, on your device. We don’t see them and cannot see them.

The site is an independent fan project, run by one person, with no ads and no data resale.

What stays on your device

The site stores in your browser’s local storage (localStorage): the works you checked and the day you checked them, the works you added yourself (images included), your settings, the path chosen on each page, your votes for the next line, and a few display preferences. An offline cache (service worker) also keeps a copy of the pages.

None of this leaves your device unless you do it yourself: an export file, a transfer link (your progress travels inside the link, with no server in between), or the Google Drive sync described below.

To erase everything, clear the site’s data in your browser settings. Files you import (Chronologeek exports, Letterboxd exports) are read in your browser and sent nowhere.

Audience measurement

We count visits with GoatCounter, an open-source tool that sets no cookies and does not keep your IP address. It records the page viewed, the referring site, screen size, browser and approximate country, without being able to identify you or follow you from site to site.

A vote for the next line is counted the same way, as an anonymous event. Vote totals are public.

An ad blocker that blocks GoatCounter breaks nothing on the site, except voting.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub receives your IP address to serve the pages and protect its service; see GitHub’s privacy statement. The site’s fonts are served by the site itself.

Services your browser calls

To show details about each work, your browser queries public services directly, so they receive your IP address and browser type, as with any image loaded on the web. We send them no data about you.

TMDB (The Movie Database): posters, synopses, trailers and streaming platforms — the latter come from JustWatch, through TMDB. RAWG: video game details and platforms. Open Library: book details. Trailers are links to YouTube: nothing loads from YouTube until you click.

The “Where to read”, “Where to watch”, Ko-fi, calendar subscription and source links lead to other sites, which have their own rules. No affiliate links are used for now; if that changes, the line concerned will say so.

Google Drive sync

If you choose to sync your progress with Google Drive, your browser loads Google’s sign-in script and asks for Google’s permission. The site only requests access to the files it creates itself (drive.file): it keeps your progress in a chronologeek.json file in your Drive, and sees none of your other files.

The access token stays in the page for the duration of the sync and is only sent to Google. Nothing is copied to a Chronologeek server: there is none. You can remove access at any time from your Google account, and delete the file from your Drive.

Chronologeek’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights and contact

Since we keep no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side: everything is on your device or in your Drive, under your control. For any question, write to us through the Contact link at the bottom of the page.
